# What others ship (checked 18 November 2026)

What Duolingo, the other language apps and the tools a traveller already carries offer, and what
Bagage lacks. Facts first, each with its source; the last section is inference. Features change
quickly: check again before deciding.

## Language apps

| App | What matters for Bagage |
| --- | --- |
| Duolingo | Speaking is now central: the share of exercises answered by speaking doubled in 2026, and Video Call (conversation with an AI character) reaches every Super subscriber by the end of 2026 [1]. Listening exercises can be skipped for a lesson (« Can't listen now ») or switched off [2]. |
| Babbel | Babbel Speak (September 2025): AI voice conversations in guided real-life scenarios, still a beta [3][4]. |
| Speak | Audio-first role-plays and an AI tutor that corrects pronunciation as you speak [5]; AI Tutor Calls for Spanish and English since October 2026 [6]. |
| Pimsleur | Audio lessons to download and play offline, a hands-free mode [7]; role-plays checked by voice recognition and an AI Conversation Coach in beta for Latin American Spanish and French [13]. |

## Tools a traveller already has

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Live Translation | Translates the other person in AirPods, processed on the iPhone; Spanish, Italian and French included, in the EU since November 2025 [8]. Needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later and recent AirPods [9]. |
| Google Translate | Live speech-to-speech conversations in over 70 languages [10]; a practice mode with listening and speaking sessions, in beta for English speakers and for learners of English only [11]; pronunciation practice on Android [12]. |

## Bagage's gaps, most useful to a traveller first

1. **Feedback on pronunciation.** Speak, Pimsleur and Google Translate check what you say; Bagage
   lets you compare your recording with the model and judge yourself.
2. **Conversation practice with an AI.** Everyone above has it. Bagage has scripted scenes.
3. **Natural voices that work offline.** Bagage uses the phone's voices: offline, but robotic on
   some devices.
4. **Quiet mode.** Shipped on 18 November 2026: an hour without listening exercises or automatic
   sound, from a button in the exercise (Duolingo's « Can't listen now ») or the speaker in the
   session bar.

Plans for 1 to 3 are in TODO.md.

## What this means (inference)

- Hearing a translation in the ear is free for many iPhone owners. « Translate for me » cannot be
  the product: Bagage's job is the confidence to speak yourself, before and during the trip, and
  to understand the reply.
- Speaking practice with feedback is expected in 2026, so it matters more than adding exercise
  types.
- Nobody above combines a trip date, a memory forecast for arrival day and offline phrases that
  work without an account. Those are worth keeping central.
